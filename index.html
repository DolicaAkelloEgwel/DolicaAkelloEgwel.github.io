About Me

Hello! 👋

My name is Dolica (it rhymes with Monica). I am part of a team of technicians within London College of Communication's Creative Technology Hub, where we work to support students in developing artworks that incorporate various forms of creative tech. The Creative Technology Hub is a space that is available to students from any degree programme and with any level of technical experience -- and we aim to create an environment that fosters experimentation and curiosity. As the Python specialist of the team, my role is centered around supporting students who wish to use Python in some shape or form to further their artistic endeavors. This typically involves delivering workshops, writing up tutorials, and providing one-to-one consultations.
